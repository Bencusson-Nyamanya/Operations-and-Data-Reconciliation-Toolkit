# Operations & Data Reconciliation Toolkit

## 📌 Overview
This repository showcases structured frameworks, data hygiene protocols, and financial record-keeping templates designed to ensure 100% accuracy in administrative and cash-flow operations. Built on practical experience in retail cash management, petty cash auditing, and real estate database administration.

---

## 📂 Toolkit Structure

### 1. Petty Cash & Cash Flow Reconciliation Log
* **Purpose:** Daily tracking and end-of-day register balancing to eliminate variances and maintain clean financial audits.
* **Key Components:** 
  * Timestamped transaction logs.
  * Category-based expense tracking (operational costs, supplies, misc).
  * Automated variance calculation check (Opening Balance + Cash In - Cash Out = Expected Closing Balance).

### 2. Client Database & Documentation Management
* **Purpose:** Maintaining clean, organized digital records for property listings and client correspondence
* **Key Practices:**
  * Standardized naming conventions for files and digital documents.
  * Regular data scrubbing protocols to eliminate duplicate entries and outdated leads.
